Admission

Enroll your child in a safe, loving, and educational environment.

How to Enroll

Enrolling your child at Almi Children and Childcare Center is simple. Visit us or contact us directly using the details below.

  • Step 1: Contact us or visit the center
  • Step 2: Fill in the registration form
  • Step 3: Child assessment (if needed)
  • Step 4: Start admission

Age Requirement

We accept children from 6 months to 10 years old.

Documents

Birth certificate or ID (if available) and parent contact details.

Working Hours

1:30 AM – 11:30 AM (Local Time)
