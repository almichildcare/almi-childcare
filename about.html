About Us

Learn more about our mission, values, and childcare philosophy.

Who We Are

Almi Children and Childcare Center is located in Debre Birhan, Ethiopia. We provide a safe, loving, and educational environment for children aged 6 months to 10 years. Our goal is to support each child's physical, emotional, social, and intellectual development.

We believe every child is unique and deserves quality care, attention, and guidance to grow into a confident and responsible individual.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality childcare and early education in a safe and nurturing environment.

Our Vision

To raise confident, creative, and responsible children prepared for future success.

Our Values

Love, care, respect, discipline, education, and child safety.

Age Groups We Care For

6 Months - 2 Years

Infant care with full attention and safety.

3 - 5 Years

Early learning, play-based education, and creativity.

6 - 10 Years

Academic support, discipline, and skill development.
